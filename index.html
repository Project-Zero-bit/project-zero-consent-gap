01 / Where the opening is

Not another pollution map.
An evidence workflow.

Public data exists. Some registers are published. Others require requests. The actual commercial problem is whether a particular decision can be backed by complete, traceable records.

01 — FIND

Locate primary sources.

Separate MOSL market discharge-point data, utility trade-effluent consents and Environment Agency water-discharge permits. They are not interchangeable.

02 — REQUEST

Ask precisely.

Generate a geographically and temporally scoped Environmental Information Regulations request. Avoid blanket demands that may be disproportionately burdensome.

03 — DOCUMENT

Record the unknown.

Create a defensible evidence-gap brief that separates verified facts, missing documentation and questions for further investigation.

05 / Method and limitations

A record is not
a verdict.

The MOSL dashboard shows market discharge-point information; an individual trade-effluent consent specifies conditions; an Environment Agency discharge permit concerns a different permitting route. We do not combine or misrepresent these datasets.

Site-level conclusions require reviewing primary records, scope, dates, context and sometimes specialist scientific or legal advice.

Known: ICO decisions have required disclosure in particular cases. Unknown: whether any specific future request will receive the same response, and whether our proposed buyers will pay for this workflow.
